## Hard requirement

> "All teams must use OpenAI Codex as their primary coding agent."

Other tools are allowed, but **Codex must play a meaningful role to be eligible
for prizes.** Two implications people miss:

1. Codex has to be the thing *building* your project.
2. The strongest possible answer to "did Codex play a meaningful role" is a
   project that is *also about Codex* — built by Codex, plugged into Codex. That
   is unarguable and it is free points.
